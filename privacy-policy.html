What Still does

Still filters incoming phone calls using the rules you choose. It processes call information and contact checks on your device. The app has no Internet permission, advertising, analytics, accounts, or developer-operated cloud service.

Information accessed on your device

Android supplies the incoming caller number to Still's call-screening service. Still uses that number to evaluate your rules. It stores the latest 200 calls it rejects, including the number, time and reason. It also stores the time and outcome of its last call check. It does not read your system call log, record calls, read SMS, or store contact names in its blocked-call history.

Optional Contacts permission lets Still check whether a number is saved in your contacts. The number tester can display the saved contact's name. With your permission, Still can also apply prefix rules to saved contacts. Contact lookups occur locally; Still does not upload your address book. Permission can be revoked in Android's app settings.

Still stores your prefixes, rule switches, pause state, preferences and blocked-call history in its private Android app storage. These records are not sent to GetonLabs. Android app backups are disabled for Still. Private app storage uses Android's app isolation; Still does not claim that it uses a separate encrypted database.

Prefix import, export and sharing

Export creates a JSON file containing only your prefix strings. It excludes contacts, blocked-call history, pause settings and rule switches. When you select a recipient through Android's share sheet, that app receives temporary read access to the chosen export. Still does not choose recipients or send files automatically.

The app or service you choose may transmit or retain the attachment under its own policies. A prefix may be a full phone number if you enter one; review the exported list before sharing sensitive entries. GetonLabs does not receive exported files unless you deliberately send one to us.

Import reads the file you choose, validates its format, and shows a review before saving. It skips duplicates and preserves existing switches. New rules are off unless you explicitly choose to enable them. File contents are treated as data and are not executed as scripts or commands. Still cannot guarantee the security of external sharing services or unrelated attachments.

Notifications, widgets and restart handling

Optional notification permission enables call-blocking controls. The widget uses your current rule and pause state. Android's restart broadcast lets Still restore opted-in controls after reboot. These features do not collect location or communicate with a server.

Retention and deletion

Blocked-call history is limited to the newest 200 rejected calls. Use Clear in Blocked calls to remove this history and the last-check status. Edit or delete prefixes in Home. Android Settings > Apps > Still > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstalling Still, removes Still's private app data.

Generated export copies may remain in private cache until Android clears it, you clear app data or uninstall, or a later export removes cached copies older than one day. Files copied to a recipient app or another storage provider are outside Still's control; delete them there if needed. Removing Still does not delete records kept by your dialer or carrier.

Support correspondence

If you email getonlabs@gmail.com, we receive the message and any information or attachments you choose to include. We use this to respond and resolve your request. Avoid sending private contacts or call history unless necessary. Correspondence is retained while needed to handle the request; you can ask us to delete it, subject to any applicable retention obligations. Email delivery is handled by the email services used by you and GetonLabs.

Children and accounts

Still does not offer account creation or features directed specifically at children. It does not knowingly collect children's information through the app. There is no Still account to delete.

Purchases

If you buy Still on Google Play, Google processes the transaction under its own policies. Still contains no in-app payment form and does not receive your payment-card details.

Policy changes

We may update this policy if Still's data practices change. The updated policy will carry a revised date. Contact getonlabs@gmail.com with questions about this policy.
