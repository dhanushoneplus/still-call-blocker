Buzzpa privacy policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026. Publisher: GetonLabs. Contact: getonlabs@gmail.com.

Buzzpa is an Android app for local alarms, notifications and simulated reminder calls. This policy describes the Buzzpa app; Still has a separate privacy policy.

Data processed on your phone

Buzzpa stores schedule titles, reminder messages, caller display names, dates, times, repeat rules, alert preferences, prepared speech audio and delivery state in its private app storage. It also keeps a small rolling local diagnostic log of delivery and calling events. These records support scheduling, alert playback, recovery after reboot and troubleshooting.

Buzzpa has no Internet permission, account system, advertising or analytics SDK. It does not upload your schedules, speech audio or diagnostic logs to GetonLabs, and does not sell or share them with advertisers. There is no GetonLabs backend for the app.

Offline speech and Android services

Reminder text is passed to your installed Android text-to-speech engine to prepare audio using an installed voice marked as offline. Buzzpa does not select a network-required voice. Installing voice data may require a separate download through your speech provider. The installed speech engine and Android system services are separate software governed by their providers' policies.

Local simulated calls use Android Telecom and your installed Phone app. Caller display text and a fixed internal Buzzpa identifier are supplied to those system components. No real telephone call is placed. Your Phone app may show these simulations in Recents, including as Unknown; those entries are outside Buzzpa's storage and control.

Permissions and access

  • Notifications show reminders, heads-up alerts, actions and delivery failures.
  • Exact-alarm access schedules alerts at chosen times. Boot and time-change events restore or adjust local schedules.
  • Full-screen alert access allows Buzzpa's alarm controls on a locked device, subject to Android behavior.
  • Phone-state access checks calling-account readiness and whether a call is already active, so a simulation does not interrupt it. Buzzpa does not request access to phone numbers, system call logs, contacts, SMS or the microphone.
  • A media-playback foreground service, wake lock and vibration support audible alarms and their controls.

You can review or revoke access in Android Settings. Revoking access may prevent the associated alerts or simulated calls.

Retention and deletion

Schedule records remain on your phone until you delete them or clear Buzzpa's data. Buzzpa cleans up unreferenced prepared audio after a grace period; audio still needed by a schedule or occurrence remains available. The diagnostic log is size-limited and older entries are replaced. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for Buzzpa.

Delete a schedule in the app to remove its record. Clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall Buzzpa to remove all of its private local data. Delete simulated-call history separately in your Phone app. No account deletion request is needed because Buzzpa has no accounts or server-side schedule store.

Support and this website

If you contact support, your email provider sends the information you choose to include to getonlabs@gmail.com. Use that address for privacy questions or requests to remove information you have sent to support. Do not include private reminder text unless necessary. Opening help or privacy links launches an external browser; this website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may process ordinary web request data under its own privacy policy. The website does not receive Buzzpa's schedule database or audio.

Changes

Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised effective date. Changes to app data practices will be reflected in the app's Play listing and this policy.
